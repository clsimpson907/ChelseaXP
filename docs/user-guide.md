# ChelseaXP User’s Guide

[Open ChelseaXP](https://chelseaxp.party)

I built ChelseaXP, my portfolio, to feel like a computer from 2006. On a desktop, **About Me** opens first. On a phone, you’ll usually start on **Today**, the Windows Mobile–style home screen.

## Using the desktop

- Open an app from its desktop shortcut or from **Start** in the lower-left corner.
- Click a window’s button on the taskbar to bring it forward. Click the active window’s taskbar button to minimize it.
- Drag a window by its title bar to move it. Drag an edge or corner to resize it. The title-bar buttons minimize, maximize or restore, and close the window.
- As Explorer and Control Panel get narrower, commands move into the **»** menus and the task sidebar can hide automatically. Widen the window to bring it back.

## Using a phone

- Tap **Owner: Chelsea Simpson** on Today to open **About Me**.
- Tap **Start → Programs** to see the app grid. Tap an icon to open it.
- Tap **Calendar** at the bottom of Today to open a month view. Use the arrows to browse months. Today and Calendar show dates as they fall in **2006**, and the clock shows your current local time.
- Tap **Today** at the bottom of an open app to return home. The **No unread messages** row is currently a placeholder.

## Finding files and folders

Start in **My Documents** for project notes and credits, or **My Computer** for an overview of the virtual computer.

- On desktop, single-click an item to select it. Double-click or press **Enter** to open it. On a phone, tap an item once to open it.
- Use **Back** to return to a previous location and **Up** to open the parent folder. Click a parent name in the address bar to jump there, or choose a destination from the dropdown and click **Go**.
- **Other Places** links to nearby folders and common destinations. **Details** shows information about the current folder or selected item. On mobile, tap **Folder tasks** to show the sidebar.
- To search with Birby, click **Search**, pick a category, enter part of a name, choose where to look, and click **Search** again.
- Open **Credits.txt** in My Documents to read it in a separate Notepad window. The résumé entry is marked **Coming soon**.
- **Local Disk (C:)** has a hidden-files screen. The floppy and CD drives ask you to insert a disk.

## Making a mess in Explorer

1. Open a folder such as **My Documents** and choose **Make a new folder** under **File and Folder Tasks**.
2. Type a name and press **Enter**. Open the new folder to create folders inside it.
3. On desktop, select a folder you made and choose **Rename this folder** or **Delete this folder**. You can also press **Delete** with an item selected. Explorer asks before deleting the folder and everything inside it.

Temporary folders are shared across open Explorer windows. A folder created in Explorer’s **Desktop** folder also appears on the visible desktop. Reloading resets all visitor-created folders. Renaming a built-in file or folder shows **Access is denied**. Deleting a built-in item asks for confirmation first. Choose **Yes** to see the error naming the item, or **No** to cancel.

## Using Control Panel

Open **Control Panel** from Start. Use the sidebar to switch between **Category View** and **Classic View**. In Category View, open **Help and Support → Complaints Department**.

## Getting help

Open **Help and Support Center** from Start or tap its row on Today. Choose a guide topic or search the guide. Under **Ask for assistance**, choose **Submit a complaint** to open the form. **Complaints Department** is under **Pick a task**.

## Sending a complaint

Open **Help and Support Center** and choose **Submit a complaint**. Enter your email address and message, then select **Send complaint**. Your name is optional. Your message goes privately to me by email. I’ll use your address if I need to reply.

A slow check or send may make the progress bar lose ground and start filling again. If a **ChelseaXP** blue screen appears, read the explanation and press a key or click or tap the screen to continue. A blue screen during the initial check closes Complaints. A quota blue screen during submission returns you to your draft.

| Blue-screen code | What it means |
|---|---|
| `HOURLY_QUOTA_FULL` | You reached your hourly limit. |
| `TEN_MINUTE_QUOTA_FULL` | Visitors collectively reached the rolling 10-minute limit. |
| `DAILY_QUOTA_FULL` | Visitors collectively reached the daily limit. |
| `COMPLAINTS_PAUSED` | Complaints is temporarily disabled. |
| `STATUS_CHECK_FAILED` | ChelseaXP couldn’t check availability. |

Quota messages give a retry time in your local time zone. By default, each visitor can send up to three complaints per rolling hour. Across all visitors, the limits are three per rolling 10 minutes and ten per UTC day.
